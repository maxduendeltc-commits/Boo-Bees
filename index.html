Resort & Logistics

Basecamp: Plaza Pelicanos Grand Beach Resort (Hotel Zone)

Transportation: Uber operates smoothly throughout PV. Local buses run frequently along Main Boulevard.

Tipping Standard: 10%–15% in restaurants/bars for good service.
