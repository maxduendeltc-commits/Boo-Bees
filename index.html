💵 Tipping & Peso Calculator

Quick reference for local tipping standards in Puerto Vallarta:

  • Restaurants: 10%–15% (Check bill for "Propina Incluida" first).
  • Taxis: Tipping is not expected, but round up $10–$20 pesos for good service.
  • Panga Drivers / Tour Guides: $50–$100 pesos per couple.
  • Baggage / Bellhops: $20–$50 pesos per bag.

El Malecón & Downtown Boardwalk
In-Town Iconic Spot

The famous oceanfront boardwalk featuring bronze sculptures, street performers, and sunset views.

🚶 Walking: ~40–45 min
🚕 Taxi / Uber: 8–12 min
🚌 Public Bus: 12–15 min
🛥️ Water Taxi: N/A
📍 Open Boardwalk Pin in Google Maps ↗
🗣️ Phrase: "¿Cómo llegamos al Malecón, por favor?" or "Al Malecón, por favor."
Zona Romántica & Los Muertos Pier
Dining & Water Taxi Hub

Historic cobblestone neighborhood full of local dining, beach clubs, and the sail-shaped Los Muertos pier where water taxis depart.

🚶 Walking: ~55–65 min
🚕 Taxi / Uber: 12–18 min
🚌 Public Bus: 20–25 min
🛥️ Water Taxi: Departure Pier
📍 Open Los Muertos Pier Pin in Google Maps ↗
🗣️ Phrase: "A la Zona Romántica / Al Muelle de Los Muertos, por favor."
Parroquia de Nuestra Señora de Guadalupe
Historical Landmark

The iconic parish church crowned with ornate wrought-iron artwork, located just one block inland from the Malecón.

🚶 Walking: ~45 min
🚕 Taxi / Uber: 10–12 min
🚌 Public Bus: 15 min
🛥️️ Water Taxi: N/A
