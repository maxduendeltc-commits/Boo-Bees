💵 Tipping & Peso Calculator

Quick reference for local tipping standards in Puerto Vallarta:

  • Restaurants: 10%–15% (Check bill for "Propina Incluida" first).
  • Taxis: Tipping is not expected, but round up $10–$20 pesos for good service.
  • Panga Drivers / Tour Guides: $50–$100 pesos per couple.
  • Baggage / Bellhops: $20–$50 pesos per bag.

Zona Romántica & Playa Los Muertos
In-Town Cultural & Dining Hub

Vibrant neighborhood south of the Cuale River full of cobblestone streets, beach clubs, craft markets, and nightlife.

🚶 Walking: ~55–60 min
🚕 Taxi / Uber: 12–18 min
🚌 Public Bus: 15–20 min
🛥️ Water Taxi: Departs Pier

Yelapa & South Coast Beaches
Day Trip / Coastal Adventure

Secluded beach cove accessible only by water taxi (panga). Features waterfalls, beachside pie ladies, and fresh seafood.

🚗 To Boca Pier: 35–45 min
🚌 Bus to Boca: ~50 min
🛥️ Water Taxi: 30–45 min
🎟️ Panga Fare: ~$120–$150 MXN
